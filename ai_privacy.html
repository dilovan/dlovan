Privacy Policy for Badini AI

Effective Date: September 18, 2026

Welcome to Badini AI. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled in a safe and responsible manner. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

To provide you with the best AI-powered conversational experience in Badini Kurdish, we collect the following types of information:

  • Audio Data (Microphone Permission): When you interact with the app using voice commands, we request access to your device's microphone. Your voice is recorded and transmitted securely to our servers to convert your speech into text and generate accurate AI responses.

Microphone Usage & Data Handling

Why we need it: The microphone permission is strictly required to enable voice interaction, allowing you to speak to the Badini AI assistant and receive verbal or textual responses.

How it works: Audio recordings are sent to our server solely for speech-to-text processing and AI response generation. We do not sell, trade, or rent your voice data to third parties.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To process your voice input and convert it into text using advanced AI models.
  • To generate relevant and accurate responses in Badini Kurdish.
  • To improve app performance, user experience, and speech recognition accuracy.

3. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your audio data and personal information during transmission and storage against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.
